Drone defense — neural-network visual detection ████ ████████ ██████ ████████ Restricted

The next war is fought by drones. The only answer that scales against a drone is another drone — detection alone does not stop anything, and ground-based effectors do not cover ground.

Comparable capability sits inside one or two Western primes. Access is allocation-gated and the technology is not pervasive. We are building the interceptor side of it, with vision on the airframe rather than in a data center.

A cloud round-trip is measured in seconds; the drone has already arrived. Tactical hardware also has to work jammed and offline. Inference runs on the airframe, at low power, with no link required — which is a silicon problem before it is a model problem.
